# WhyCSatUICUnity

**SparkHacks 2026 — Track 2**

Many of our teammates are first-generation college students. Growing up, we had little guidance on which college to attend or what major to choose. We would’ve greatly benefited from a fun, interactive way to learn more about Computer Science at UIC.

Graduation was always the goal—everyone in our lives made that clear—but *how* to get there? We had no clue.

To be the change we wanted to see, our group created a helpful and welcoming experience that gives students insight into what college life and studying CS at UIC can look like.
